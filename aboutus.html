About Droplet

One drop at a time.

How it started

Droplet began with a simple idea: a small, calm place in Karachi where people can slow down and enjoy a good cup of coffee.

We wanted a cafe that feels like a quiet corner of home, with warm drinks, fresh bakes and time to talk. Every cup is made with care, one drop at a time.

What we believe in

Made with care

Good ingredients

Fresh beans, real milk and bakes made daily, so every sip tastes right.
